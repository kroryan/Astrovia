# Privacy Policy — Astrovia

_Last updated: 2026-10-15_

Astrovia is a free, open-source application without ads, analytics or user accounts.

**Data we collect:** none. Astrovia does not collect, store on any server, or share personal data.

**Network requests:** to display sky images and catalogs, the app connects directly from your device
to public astronomical services (HiPS survey servers, SIMBAD and VizieR operated by the CDS, Strasbourg).
These requests include the sky coordinates/object names you look at or search for, and — as with any
internet connection — your IP address is visible to those servers. Their own privacy policies apply.

**Data stored on your device:** settings, a tile cache and any HiPS surveys / catalogs you choose to
download. You can clear the cache from Settings or by uninstalling the app.

**Permissions:** `INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC`
and `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`. The last three exist only so that a download you start (HiPS tiles or a
catalogue) keeps running while the app is in the background and shows its progress in a notification
with a Stop button. They are not used for advertising, tracking or any message from us.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

**Contact:** open an issue at <https://github.com/kroryan/Astrovia/issues>.
